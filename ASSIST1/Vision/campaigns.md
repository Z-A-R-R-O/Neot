# Campaigns — Spec

## Overview

Campaigns allow users to organize posts around a theme, goal, or time period. Users can plan, schedule, and track campaign performance across platforms.

## Campaign Structure

```
Campaign
├── Name: "Summer Product Launch"
├── Goal: "Drive 100 pre-orders"
├── Date Range: Jun 1 - Jun 30
├── Status: Active
├── Platforms: Instagram, LinkedIn, X
│
├── Posts (12)
│   ├── Jun 1: "Teaser" → Instagram, X
│   ├── Jun 5: "Feature #1" → LinkedIn
│   ├── Jun 10: "Behind the scenes" → Instagram
│   ├── Jun 15: "Launch day!" → All platforms
│   └── ...
│
└── Performance
    ├── Impressions: 45,000
    ├── Engagement: 3,200
    ├── Link clicks: 890
    └── Conversions: 34
```

## Campaign Builder

### Step 1: Setup
- Campaign name
- Goal (awareness, engagement, traffic, conversions, sales)
- Date range
- Platforms
- Budget (optional — for ad tracking later)

### Step 2: Content Planning
- Create posts within campaign
- Drag to arrange timeline
- Bulk-create from AI ("Generate a 2-week launch campaign")
- Templates for common campaign types

### Step 3: Review & Launch
- Calendar view of all campaign posts
- Platform-specific previews
- Approval workflow (optional)
- Launch campaign → posts are scheduled

## Campaign Dashboard

### Overview
- Active campaigns count
- Campaign performance summary
- Upcoming campaign posts

### Detail View
- Timeline/Gantt chart of posts
- Performance metrics per post
- Platform breakdown
- Goal progress tracker
