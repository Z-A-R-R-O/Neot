# Business Model — Spec

## Overview

AI Social OS is a SaaS platform with a free tier and paid upgrades. The business model is designed for recurring revenue with clear expansion paths.

## Pricing Tiers

| Feature | Free | Pro ($29/mo) | Agency ($99/mo) |
|---------|------|--------------|-----------------|
| Connected accounts | 2 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Posts per month | 30 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| AI generations/mo | 50 | 500 | Unlimited |
| Analytics retention | 30 days | 90 days | 1 year |
| Team members | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Auto-replies | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| White-label | — | — | ✓ |
| API access | — | — | ✓ |
| Priority support | — | — | ✓ |

## Revenue Streams

### Primary: SaaS Subscriptions
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Annual discount (2 months free)
- Usage-based add-ons for heavy AI users

### Secondary: Agency Model
- Agency subscription ($199/mo) includes:
  - Up to 50 managed client accounts
  - White-label dashboard
  - Client reporting
  - Bulk operations

### Future: Marketplace
- Content template marketplace
- AI prompt marketplace
- Integration marketplace (Canva, Stripe, etc.)
- Revenue share: 70/30 (creator/platform)

## Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1 — Organic (0-500 users)
- Product-led growth
- Free tier with generous limits
- "Built for creators" positioning
- Twitter/LinkedIn content about building in public
- Directory listings (Product Hunt, SaaStr, G2)

### Phase 2 — Content (500-2,000 users)
- SEO blog (social media tips, AI content guides)
- Comparison pages (vs Buffer, vs Hootsuite)
- Template gallery (content calendars, campaign plans)
- YouTube tutorials

### Phase 3 — Paid (2,000+ users)
- Targeted ads (freelancers, agencies)
- Partnership with creator economy tools
- Agency referral program (10% recurring commission)
