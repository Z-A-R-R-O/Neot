### Recommendations Engine Improvement (Z-01: Recommendation Engine)
- `src/lib/courses/recommendations.ts` — complete rewrite with scoring system:
  - Fetches 50 candidate courses instead of separate queries
  - Scores each course based on:
    - **Category affinity** (+10 per matching top category)
    - **Interest matching** (+5 per interest match in tags/title/subject)
    - **Popularity** (+0.5 per enrollment, capped at 20)
    - **Recency** (decay over 30 days, max +5)
  - Returns top N by score
  - Reads user interests from Profile.metadata
  - Filters out deleted courses
