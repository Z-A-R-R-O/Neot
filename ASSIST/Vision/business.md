# Business Models — Spec

## Revenue Streams

### Subscription Tiers

| Tier | Price | Target | Features |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Students trying platform | Basic courses, 5 enrollments, limited storage |
| Pro | $9.99/mo | Serious learners | Unlimited courses, AI tutor, analytics, certificates |
| School | Custom | K-12, colleges | White-label, bulk licenses, admin tools, SLA |
| Enterprise | Custom | Corporations | API access, custom integrations, dedicated support |

### Marketplace

- Teacher-created courses (revenue share 70/30)
- Premium content library
- Template marketplace for teachers
- Plugin/extensions marketplace (future)

### Add-ons

- Extra storage: $2/mo per 10GB
- Priority support: $5/mo
- Custom certificates: $1/issue
- Advanced analytics: $3/mo

## Customer Acquisition

- Freemium model (free tier drives adoption)
- School partnerships (bulk deals)
- Teacher referrals (commission on Pro upgrades)
- Content marketing (free resources, blog)
- App store optimization

## Cost Structure

| Category | % Revenue | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Infrastructure | 15% | Hosting, CDN, AI API costs |
| Development | 40% | Engineering team |
| Marketing | 20% | Acquisition, partnerships |
| Support | 10% | Customer service |
| Margin | 15% | Profit, reinvestment |
